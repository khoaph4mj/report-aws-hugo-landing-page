### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment during my internship was comfortable and suitable for learning, especially since this was my first experience working in a professional environment. The team members were friendly and always willing to support me whenever I had questions or difficulties. One thing that could be improved is the communication process, as most discussions were through online chat, which sometimes caused delays in receiving responses. Having short regular check-in sessions could help improve coordination and progress tracking.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
My mentor provided valuable support throughout the internship and was always willing to spend time explaining concepts whenever I needed guidance. I especially appreciated that my mentor encouraged me to learn from mistakes instead of expecting everything to be completed perfectly from the beginning. This helped me become more confident and improve my problem-solving skills. Administrative support, such as document preparation and internship confirmations, was also handled efficiently.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
Some internship tasks were related to my academic background, especially programming concepts and database management. However, many aspects, particularly cloud infrastructure, AWS services, and deployment processes, were new areas that I had not studied deeply before. Learning these technologies required additional effort, but it also helped me expand my knowledge beyond the university curriculum.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
This internship provided me with opportunities to work with technologies and tools that I had limited experience with before. Besides improving technical knowledge, I also learned how to explain technical problems more clearly, communicate progress effectively, and approach problems from different perspectives by observing my teammates' working methods.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The team had a supportive and collaborative working culture. Whenever I faced difficulties or needed more time to understand a new topic, teammates were willing to guide me and discuss possible solutions. This created a comfortable environment where I felt encouraged to ask questions and continuously improve myself.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The internship allowance and flexible working schedule were helpful during the internship period. In addition, having access to internal learning resources and training sessions provided more opportunities for me to explore new technologies outside of my assigned tasks.

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
One of the most meaningful moments during my internship was when I became able to understand and analyze a cloud architecture diagram by myself. Compared to the beginning of the internship, when cloud concepts were still unfamiliar, this showed me the progress I had made throughout the learning process.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
The company could consider assigning each intern an additional support member or buddy who has a similar level of experience. This would allow interns to ask smaller questions more comfortably while reducing the workload on mentors.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would recommend this internship program, especially for students who want to explore cloud computing and gain practical experience. The supportive environment allows interns to learn from mistakes and gradually improve their skills.

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
Providing a list of recommended learning resources before the internship starts, such as basic AWS concepts, common tools, and required technical knowledge, would help new interns prepare better and adapt faster during the first weeks.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
I would consider continuing with FCAJ in the future if there is a suitable opportunity and schedule. The internship provided me with valuable experience and motivated me to continue developing my skills in cloud computing and software engineering.

**Any other comments (free sharing):**  
I would like to sincerely thank my mentor and teammates for their patience and support throughout the internship. Even when I was still catching up with some new concepts, everyone was willing to help and encouraged me to keep learning and improving.
